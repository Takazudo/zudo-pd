# Print the straight adhesive PCB legs

Units: mm. Import at 100% scale.

- adhesive-leg-18mm-m3.stl: one leg; print four copies.
- adhesive-leg-18mm-m3-4x.stl: four separate legs on one plate.

Place the broad flat bases on the bed, with the screw openings upward. Use the
material and hole-compensation settings that worked for the tested rail.
No supports are needed.

Each leg is 18 mm high, with a 14 × 14 × 2 mm base and a straight Ø7 mm pole.
The hole preserves the exact 27-vertex rail profile, stopping at 4.5 mm depth.
There is no lower cavity and no top taper. Material below the hole is solid.

Use M3 × 5 mm non-countersunk screws from the PCB back. A 1.6 mm PCB leaves
3.4 mm insertion and 1.1 mm nominal space to the blind floor. Alternatively,
M3 × 6 mm with a washer at least 0.5 mm thick gives at most 3.9 mm insertion.
The previous M3 × 8 mm screws are too long for this revision.

Fit all four legs before applying adhesive to the flat undersides. Existing PCB
holes remain nominally 3.0 mm; confirm the actual M3 screws pass freely through
them. The four bases need 116 × 91 mm of mounting space, extending 3 mm beyond
each PCB edge. Printed retention and adhesive grip need the actual hardware check.

# LED Lamp

The lamp is the shared [P15 r1](/docs/shared-p15/) 15 V / 3 A input with Board L (LED drive, MCU control), its
firmware and a printed enclosure. Its source of record is
[`products/led-lamp/`](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-pd/tree/main/products/led-lamp), imported from
`zudo-led-lamp` with its history. This page owns the current LED status; the pages under it are historical LED
pages unless stated otherwise.

## Current source

| Part | Current source | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input | Shared P15 r1, owned by `boards/board-p15/` ([Shared P15](/docs/shared-p15/)). The LED consumer reference is `products/led-lamp/boards/p15-r1-reference.json` | Not ordered, programmed or measured |
| Board L | `products/led-lamp/boards/board-l/` as ordered on 2026-09-19, reused unchanged. RS1 = 200 mΩ (RLP25FEER200) | 2 received; not inspected; MCU believed blank; RS1 not yet measured |
| Firmware | `products/led-lamp/firmware/board-l/target/`, V72 sources, `CTRL_FULL_CCR` 2407 (75.2 %) | Build-only UNFLASHED BENCH CANDIDATE |
| Enclosure | `products/led-lamp/enclosure/` prototype 03 generator and its tracked print STLs | CAD fit with P15 at nominal datums only |

RS1 = 200 mΩ with `CTRL_FULL_CCR` 2407 is the adopted pairing. The 250 mΩ rework and the 3200 ceiling are not
selected; HUSB238 and new animation behaviour are not adopted. The old LED `board-p/` pages describe historical
15 V hardware, distinct from P15 and from PD20's Board P r3.

## Firmware candidate

The 2026-11-20 build at LED `dd4cb00` (the same firmware tree as this import) is an **UNFLASHED BENCH
CANDIDATE**: loadable image SHA-256 `421e5b6d5626355170491edf257844d774d93b3c89e32ab20f3982cd1fec2a9e`,
10908 B, profile `default-rs1-200mohm`. Host tests passed (44, 5430, 106 and 71053 checks). The receipt is
[`firmware/board-l/receipts/2026-11-20-unflashed-bench-candidate.md`](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-pd/blob/main/products/led-lamp/firmware/board-l/receipts/2026-11-20-unflashed-bench-candidate.md);
no binary image is committed. The current image runs steady and linear (noise NULL, no gamma) and does not read
PDOK/ATT. Noise, gamma, latch and knob/NTC tuning values and PD gating remain open owner decisions in
[`OPEN-PRODUCT-DECISIONS.md`](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-pd/blob/main/products/led-lamp/firmware/board-l/target/OPEN-PRODUCT-DECISIONS.md).

## Enclosure print inputs

Print from the tracked STLs and `smoke-reversing-stls.zip` listed with their hashes in
[`PRINT-INPUTS.md`](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-pd/blob/main/products/led-lamp/candidates/p15-r1-enclosure-fit/PRINT-INPUTS.md).
They are valid for P15 at the default parameters. The Bambu `_3dp/*.3mf` projects lack 7 of the 8 fitting
coupons, so print those from the STLs first.

## Preparation and post-arrival sequence

[Lamp prototype preparation](./prototype-preparation.md) lists the jig, instruments and parts and the order of
steps after P15 arrives: P15 inspection and NVM acceptance, Board L unpowered checks and RS1 Kelvin
measurement, first energizing, option-byte readback, firmware identity, current and thermal checks, mating
and enclosure fit. Every programming, energizing or fit step needs the owner's explicit permission.

## Not run

P15 ordering, programming and PD acceptance; Board L inspection, RS1 measurement, energizing, option-byte
access, flashing and readback; LED current, thermal and NTC measurements; enclosure printing and physical fit
are all **NOT RUN**. The 200 mΩ derate-onset temperature is not computed (UNKNOWN).

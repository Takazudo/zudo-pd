## The required specification is kept for every adopted part

- **Precision resistors.** All seven UNI-ROYAL parts carry the full `T5F` suffix: ±0.1 %, ±25 ppm/°C, 0.1 W at 70 °C,
  75 V and −55 to 155 °C. The datasheet load-life limits match the previous RT parts' in the compared rows, but the test
  conditions are not all the same; an initial 0.1 % part is not claimed to stay within 0.1 % at end of life. R35
  (33 Ω) keeps its precision because the limiter window 1.94204–1.99556 A and floor 1.92899 A depend on it.
- **C0G capacitors.** 150 pF and 1.5 nF stay C0G/NP0, 5 %, 50 V, 0603. C6 (47 pF) is retained; C105622 is recorded as
  its backup only.
- **JSCJ zeners.** A shared SOD-123 name does not make the parts compatible. The adopted parts use a
  manufacturer-neutral footprint whose pads are identical to the previous land, and the thermal screen was kept. The
  dominating case, P D9, stays at or below 88.314 mW at 22.56 V and 139.463 mW for an assumed continuous 28.35 V, including
  initial resistor tolerance and a 100 ppm/°C sensitivity. JSCJ's 250 °C/W is a test-board value, not this PCB's thermal
  resistance; 125 °C ambient combined with continuous 28.35 V is outside the screen, and no ambient rating is created.
  The JSCJ temperature coefficients are typical columns and are recorded as typical.
- **INA301.** The Q1 A3 variant keeps the gain, pins, gain error 0.2 %, VOS 35 µV, LIMIT 79.2–80.8 µA, and the 1 µs
  and 1.5 µs delays with their conditions; RESET stays at GND, OUT is NC and the ALERT wiring is unchanged. A1, A2,
  INA381 and INA300 change the circuit or response and are not BOM substitutes. 13 units in stock is not large stock.

## Comparison cost is +$1.6274 for two pairs, not an order total

Using the price tier for each board job's placement count, with PD20 assumed at two pairs only for comparison:

- INA301: 4 × ($3.6223 − $2.7241) = +$3.5928.
- The other 11 changes: −$1.9654.
- Total versus the previous set: **+$1.6274**, mounted parts only.

This assumes the previous zero-stock parts could be bought at their old price. It excludes extra purchase quantity,
minimums, loss, setup, assembly, PCB, shipping and tax. It is not added to or subtracted from the earlier $527.34 catalog
estimate. The PD20 quantity is undecided. Under Standard PCBA the $1.53 setup charge applies per unique SMT part for both
Basic and Extended parts, so a Basic-to-Extended change is not itself a cost increase.

## Adoption and package state

The adoption updated the component owners, inventory, pin locks, symbols, the neutral SOD-123 footprint and model,
integration rules and forward test, the generator specs, the Board P and B20 schematics and PCBs, and the publication
selection. The Board P r3 rebuild and B20 field sync changed only the library identity of the zener footprints and
the adopted Value and LCSC fields. Native DRC has 0 errors and 0 unconnected items on both boards.

Manufacturing outputs for the new part set are generated in a new destination,
`manufacturing/releases/pd20-sourcing-review-20261122/`; `pd20-prototype-review/` and `corner-support-review/` are kept
unchanged. See [current state](../project/current-state.md) for the package and check status.

Electrical acceptance is NOT ESTABLISHED and T1–T8 are NOT RUN. Before an order, re-check the adopted codes' stock,
INA301 in particular, at the real quantity.

PD20 is the separate **20 V / 5 A** product for the OSC, with a 5 A e-marked cable and **no degraded mode**.
Its pair is **`board-p + board-b20-draft`**. [Current state](../project/current-state.md#pd20-board-p-r3-plus-b20)
owns current implementation and physical status; [release gates](./release-readiness.md) owns acceptance.

AP63201/LM2596/LDO chain and 15 V binary are not PD20 instructions.

## Electrical interface

B20's P1 row is **NC, PDOK, GND, NC**; it has no ATT. TP3/TP4/TP5 are P12_PRE/P5_PRE/M12_PRE.
Mechanical positions match Board B but electrical functions differ. Validate the r3/B20 net mapping against
its executable definitions before mating; shared hole and header positions do not authorize cross-pairing.

The OSC continuous contract is +12 / −12 / +5 V at 1.7 / 1.6 / 0.3 A; 20 ms transient targets are
2.0 / 1.9 / 0.4 A; let-through maxima including tolerance are 2.1 / 2.0 / 0.5 A.
These are acceptance demands, not measured output capabilities.

## Evidence boundary

The [original independent B20 review](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-pd/blob/main/manufacturing/pd20-b20-layout-review.md)
and [current disposition](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-pd/blob/main/manufacturing/pd20-status.md) are separate records.
Later fixes address Kelvin sensing, returns and thermal spreading; retained SMC diode loop area and limiter-maximum
thermal behavior still need EMI/thermal measurement. C43 is implemented; its DC-bias and loop-inductance limits remain.

Board P r3 Q1 soft start is reopened (2026-11-11). The adopted `dec-p-20v-q1-soft-start` slew of 3.1–10 V/ms assumes
C36 at 0.4–1.1 µF. Samsung typical DC-bias data for the exact part gives 0.14–0.18 µF at the start of the 20 V closure,
so a calculated typical peak slew of about 19–31 V/ms and peak inrush of 3.6–7.6 A exceed the adopted bound and U2's
I(OL) minimum, and the Q1 SOA margin is not established. The design is unchanged; this is an open finding
([evidence](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-pd/blob/main/manufacturing/pd20-evidence-research.md), readiness D10).

The [original P r3 review](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-pd/blob/main/boards/board-p/r3-draft/independent-review.md) is preserved with its November 8 findings. R1–R4 geometry, the B20 U6/C35 correction and final probe/polarity artwork received bounded dispositions with native evidence and [independent correction review](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-pd/blob/main/boards/board-p/r3-draft/independent-correction-review.md), integrated in [PR #240](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-pd/pull/240). R5 turn-off/undershoot (T7) and R7 conditional SOA (T3) stay open, as do installed thermal resistance, via/stencil process, component-source/repetitive-pulse gaps, sequencing, let-through and physical tests.
The [canonical OSC contract](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-osc-hole-field/blob/433c88b27b2e49284fe1a717b47ba4226c829dd3/design/architecture-278/integration/power-contract/pd-interface-20v.md)
was inspected at OSC revision `433c88b27b2e49284fe1a717b47ba4226c829dd3` on 2026-11-09
(file SHA-256 `8702890d4d13c4ba3fbd129dca98ccabf12b006aee7e99230e81ed4dad09556a`).
Its let-through maxima include overshoot: a limiter trip threshold does not establish compliance.
Loaded asymmetric discharge and shutdown/re-entry remain pre-order interface gates. The contract calls for
output disconnection before discharge; any tracked ramp-down before disconnection needs an explicit agreement.
The OSC reverse-leakage target is still proposed, while PD has adopted it; discharge-time backdrive needs
agreed timing semantics. No acceptable over-limit charge/time budget has been established.
The phase33 analysis tools now reject incomplete inputs and use the contract's 1.5 V hold threshold;
synthetic regression tests do not replace circuit simulation or physical qualification.

**No program-ready 20 V binary exists.** TDISPAR0V position and initial lower-current source matching are documented. The selected value/reset interaction, POWER_ONLY_ABOVE_5V mapping conflict and established-contract transition behavior remain unresolved.
Use [PD20 verification](../verification/pd20-verification.md); do not use the P15 recipe or intent JSON to provision r3.

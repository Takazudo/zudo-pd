This page is the project's sourcing policy. It explains how parts are chosen for supply and cost, and how supply
observations are recorded. It is not a replacement for the canonical component inventory
(`.claude/skills/component-spec-audit/references/inventory.json`) or for a generated assembly BOM. Nothing on this page
is an order, a quote, a stock reservation or an assembly plan.

The latest dated audit under this policy is the
[parts sourcing audit of 2026-11-22 JST](../research/parts-sourcing-audit-20261122.mdx), with the machine-readable
[101-line ledger](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-pd/blob/main/manufacturing/readiness/parts-sourcing-audit-20261122.json).

## Purpose and scope

Among the parts that satisfy the required electrical, thermal, mechanical and manufacturing conditions, choose one that
is easy to obtain in the needed quantity and keeps the whole order cost low. A generic part with zero or low stock is the
first place to look for an equivalent with more stock.

The current scope is PD20 (Board P r3 and Board B20) and the planned additional P15 boards. Earlier Board B, Board L,
received hardware and frozen packages are separate history and are not rewritten by a sourcing change.

A cheap, well-stocked part in use is still compared and the comparison is recorded, but saving a few cents is not by
itself a reason to replace a good part. Count the cost of another unique part, its verification, another sourcing route
and a split order line.

## Decision order

1. Fix the part's role in the circuit and its required specification.
2. Identify the exact manufacturer, the complete MPN with suffix, the supplier code, and the board and references.
3. Compare the part in use with each candidate from primary sources: specification, pin numbering and polarity,
   recommended land pattern, outline and mounted height.
4. Judge supply with the same basis for every candidate: the board quantity, placements per board, the side split, the
   sourcing route, assembly loss and minimum quantities.
5. Compare cost as the needed purchase quantity plus setup, manual or special processes, shipping and lead time, not unit
   price alone.
6. Record each line as adopted, retained, retained with a backup candidate, a redesign-only candidate, or retained
   pending a supply check.

A candidate whose comparison basis is unconfirmed stays unconfirmed. A catalog title or a shared package name does not
make a part compatible. A typical value is never used as a guaranteed limit.

## Conditions by part type

- **General resistors.** Keep resistance, tolerance, any required TCR, power, voltage, temperature range and outline. A
  0 Ω part is compared by its residual resistance and current rating, not its printed tolerance.
- **Precision resistors.** Keep the precision budget of that reference. Do not relax the ±0.1 % / 25 ppm/°C parts used
  here to 0.5 %, 1 % or 50 ppm/°C for price. Treat initial tolerance, temperature drift and long-term drift
  separately; check load life, damp heat and soldering heat where they matter. An initial 0.1 % part does not guarantee
  0.1 % at end of life. Scaling a divider while keeping its ratio is a design change: input bias, RC and leakage change.
- **Capacitors.** Compare dielectric, effective capacitance at the applied voltage, ESR, ripple current, temperature,
  life and recommended land as well as nominal value, tolerance and rating. Never replace a timing or compensation C0G
  with X7R. Two 10 µF / 50 V / 1210 parts with different DC-bias curves are not equivalent.
- **Inductors.** Compare inductance and DCR, the saturation definition and its test condition, temperature rise, maximum
  temperature, winding voltage, and the waveform and fault current of each placement. Do not move a switching inductor
  and a post-filter inductor together because they share a package.
- **Shunts.** Keep resistance, tolerance, TCR, Kelvin connection, current and pulse ratings, the copper condition of
  the power rating, and the land. A catalog "1 W" includes its board condition.
- **Semiconductors.** Compare pins, supply range, leakage, on-resistance, gain, offset, delay, protection behavior, surge
  rating, thermal condition and recommended land for the role the part plays. INA301 A1/A2/A3, same-name TVS parts
  from different manufacturers, and TPS54560 versus TPS54560B are never treated as the same part automatically.
- **Connectors.** Keep pitch and also per-pin rating, orientation, key, mating part, holes and retention, and stack height.

## Stock and quantity

Every stock or price observation records its retrieval time and time zone, supplier, URL, MPN, supplier code and price
tier. A stock value is an observation at that time, not a reservation or a lead-time promise. LCSC stock, the JLC
public pool, JLC consigned or private stock, and Global Sourcing availability are kept apart and never added together.

The JLC API fields `minPurchaseNum`, `leastPatchNumber` and `lossNumber` are stored as separate observations. None of
them is "the MOQ". JLC's public help describes different conditions for buying an in-stock Extended part, pre-ordering an
out-of-stock part, and the minimum and loss quantities of assembly. One API field does not determine the quantity a
Standard PCBA order will bill. Stock below `minPurchaseNum` alone does not prove a part cannot be placed.

The needed quantity comes from the actual board count and the placements on each side. The usable quantity and loss are
confirmed in the quote and order screens. When a minimum or loss quantity is unconfirmed, the line is recorded as supply
unconfirmed. The PD20 "two pairs" comparison basis is not changed into an order quantity.

## Cost

On 2026-11-22 JST the JLC public price list gave a Standard PCBA SMT feeder setup charge of **$1.53 per unique SMT part
for both Basic and Extended parts**, and $3.07 per unique Extended part for Economic PCBA. The charge is per unique part,
not per placement or per board. The Preferred Extended waiver is an Economic condition. PD20 is two-sided Standard PCBA,
so a change from a Basic to an Extended part is not by itself a cost increase. Through-hole parts are handled separately
from SMT feeders. Recheck these amounts and conditions at the next quote.

A "unit price at the 20-piece tier" or a "difference times placements" is a comparison metric. Name it as such; it is not
a purchase cost or a finished quote. State which costs are included: parts, setup, PCB, two-sided assembly, inspection
and fixtures, packing, shipping and tax. Do not subtract a unit-price difference from an older catalog estimate to make a
new order total.

## Evidence and adoption

The audit ledger keeps one line per part code: boards and references, role, required specification, the part in use,
compared candidates, retrieval times, price tiers and quantity fields, primary sources (title, revision, URL, SHA256), the
reason for the decision, and the conditions left. "Researched" and "adopted" are different states.

Adopting a part updates, together and consistently: the component owner bundle, the inventory, facts and sources, pin
map, footprint and model, integration rules, the generator spec, schematic, PCB, BOM and CPL, and the publication
selection. Candidates do not enter the published inventory first. Source bytes go to the private
`Takazudo/circuit-dev-resources` repository; this repository records metadata and digests. Generated component pages and
catalogs are never edited by hand.

When the same code stays on an older board or on P15, the adoption names its scope per board and reference. Never
replace a string across the whole repository. Frozen releases and past checksums and receipts are kept; after a change,
manufacturing data is generated as a new revision in a new destination.

Redo electrical calculations, native KiCad, DRC, refill and parity, STEP datum review, assembly preview and Gerber, BOM
and CPL verification as the change requires. A PASS of the earlier package does not carry over to the new part set.
Adopting a part, consistent manufacturing files and measured electrical acceptance are three different states.

## Build reconciliation and ongoing use

The component-selection step of [circuit/WORKFLOW.md](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-pd/blob/main/circuit/WORKFLOW.md)
links here. [Current state](../project/current-state.md), the
[order readiness ledger](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-pd/blob/main/manufacturing/prototype-order-readiness.md) and
[release readiness](../architecture/release-readiness.md) also link here. Dated audits go under
[research](../research/index.mdx); machine-readable ledgers go under `manufacturing/readiness/`.

Immediately before an order, re-check each adopted part's exact code, stock, price, needed quantity, side placement,
supply route and the hash of the latest manufacturing package. A request to research, document, change the design or
merge is not permission to buy, reserve stock, request a quote, contact a supplier, send manufacturing files, program a
part or energize hardware.

A USB-PD powered modular synthesizer power supply: USB-C PD 15 V in, +12 V/1.2 A,
−12 V/0.8 A and +5 V/0.5 A out. The design is split across two boards — **Board A**
negotiates and switches the 15 V rail, **Board B** converts it into the three synth
rails. Start at [Project Overview](./overview.md).

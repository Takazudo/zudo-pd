# HC-11 corner-support evidence

The selected accessory is [Tayda A-2882 / Haidebao HC-11](https://www.taydaelectronics.com/hc-11-11mm-pcb-support-html.html). Its [linked drawing](https://www.taydaelectronics.com/datasheets/images/A-2882.jpg) specifies a **3.0 mm hole** and **1.6 mm PCB/chassis thickness**. The page assigns **A = 11 mm**. The unmodified image and its hash are retained beside [hc11-evidence.json](./hc11-evidence.json).

The drawn A arrows run from the base underside to the PCB seating shoulder. This interpretation includes the base height and excludes the retaining head above the board. Tape thickness, compression and the precise bonded-floor datum are not separately specified. The drawing also labels a 2.0 mm retention region and a 2.1 mm narrow tip width; the latter is not a diameter. Base, column and complete head dimensions and tolerances are missing. No raster-scaled dimensions or invented manufacturer CAD are used.

`MountingHole_HC11_3mm` is a separate 3.0 mm NPTH footprint for H4–H7. H1–H3 retain their 3.2 mm M3 interface with Board P. Its 6 mm Fab circle and 6.5 mm square courtyard are **project clearance assumptions**, inherited from the existing hole pattern, and do not establish HC-11 collar or adhesive-base dimensions. Both canonical copies are in `footprints/kicad/` and the actual bound library `footprints/kicad/zudo-power.pretty/`. It is board-only and excluded from BOM and placement output. Four supports are separately procured mechanical hardware.

**The 11 mm support does not clear the current front-down assembly above a flat enclosure floor.** With the Board B component face toward the adhesive floor, the P component face is 11.1 mm from B and P's 1.6 mm substrate reaches 12.7 mm: already 1.7 mm beyond the nominal floor plane. The screw terminal is 14.0 mm nominal, 14.2 mm at the drawing's height maximum, exceeding that plane by 3.0–3.2 mm. P solder tails may extend farther. These are geometric conflicts under the stated orientation; missing physical measurements do not make them passing margins.

A taller support or actual floor clearance is required. A proposed 16 mm support would leave 1.8 mm beyond the terminal drawing maximum before seating tolerances and other geometry, but no alternate support is selected or qualified here. Moving P only in X/Y cannot solve a flat-floor height conflict. Exact supports, finished holes, retention, adhesive loading, wire access and the finished enclosure remain physical fit checks.

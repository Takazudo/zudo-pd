## E23 as recorded
The 2.1 / 2.0 / 0.5 A maxima are steady-state limits. Per fault event and per rail, the current may exceed the maximum
only within this allowance: time above the maximum ≤ 0.25 ms, peak ≤ 130 A, I²t ≤ 0.1 A²s.

E23's conditions:
1. One event per power cycle. Any trip latches an all-rail shutdown with no automatic retry; a retry or hiccup mode
   reopens the row.
2. The figures are PD SIM until a bench detection-to-off measurement replaces them (OSC #57).
3. The allowance is re-checked against the inlet connector and cable once selected, including a re-run of the short
   with the selected cable.
4. Shutdown order, discharge and skew rows, the E21 / E22 definitions and the trip points are unchanged.

## PD-side reading of the conditions
- **Condition 1, as drawn:** FLT_L is set by the trip chain and cleared only by the power-on reset (Q32 from POR_H,
  `board_b20_spec.py`). A trip therefore holds EN_E low through Q39 until P5_SUP cycles. Nothing in the drawn circuit
  retries. This is connectivity only, not a bench result.
- **Condition 2:** the bench detection-to-off measurement joins the hands-on track. It is not run.
- **Condition 3:** the cable and inlet re-run is owed when they are selected. The short deck takes the cable as one
  line (`LFLT` and the 20 mΩ term in `d3_let_through.py`).
- **Condition 4:** nothing on the PD side changes.

# T2 (a2) DC checks: DT2K sees at most 18.34 V and the 30 V figure was a 2× margin on an underestimate; DT2C stays within 10.6 V even with D_L open; the window switch must move to the inverting input

**Owner decisions on this paper (2026-11-12), recorded here; the sections below are kept as written:**
- DT2K: a stated rating of at least 27.5 V (1.5 × 18.34 V). BAS116LT1G is bound (75 V). FJH1100 is DT2C only.
- The 5 nA DT2K figure is withdrawn: it was a hold-droop budget with an overstated basis. DT2K leakage is decided
  by the 80 nA (150 °C row) simulation case. That rests on the flat 80 nA bound, not the log-linear chord.
- The window-switch fix (t2m pulled toward M12_PRE through 100 k) is accepted, subject to the VCM / abs-max table
  in `d1-t2-rev2-criteria.md`.
- The hot case uses the board maximum operating temperature, not the 125 °C proposed below. The repo states none,
  and the owner is asking Takazudo.
- The 37–46 µA limiter current consuming no D3 margin is accepted.

Paper only. Nothing is pre-registered or dispatched, and D1 stays on hold.

Sources for the bounds:
- P5_SUP is 4.9259–5.0423 V (calculator `p5_sup_v`, 25 °C tolerance corners).
- |M12_PRE| is at most 12.3 V, the calculator's own pessimistic bound (`m12_pre = -(SOURCE_BAND['-12'][1] + 0.1)`).
- OPA2187 (C2863562, owner `component-board-b20-draft-candidates`): signal inputs are limited to the rails ±0.5 V. The only VCM row retained is (V−) − 0.1 V to (V+) − 2 V, for VS 4.5 to < 8 V.
- Deck connectivity is `tracking/adv.cir`, and FJH1100 rows come from the staged evidence (`fjh1100-evidence.json`).

## DT2K: worst-case reverse voltage is 18.34 V, rail-bounded; (a2) does not change it
DT2K's anode is DRV (U9B output) and its cathode is t2k, which the capture switch connects to the hold (t2h, C_H to M12_PRE).

| Case | Bound | VR |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal operation | The hold charges only through DT2K from DRV, so it is at most DRV's maximum, P5_SUP. DRV is at least M12_PRE | ≤ 5.0423 + 12.3 = **17.34 V** |
| Power sequencing / unpowered | The XT2A input and the U9B output are clamped to their rails ±0.5 V. If M12_PRE collapses while C_H holds charge, t2h rises with it until XT2A's input clamp conducts | ≤ 17.34 + 1.0 = **18.34 V** |
| U3 loses regulation (\|M12_PRE\| grows) | Not bounded by any board protection. The same span exceeds the OPA2187 40 V supply absolute maximum of U9, U15, U16 and XT2A/B at about \|M12_PRE\| 35 V | Outside the board's own fault boundary; not covered |

**Where 30 V came from.** It is my own earlier line (requirements note, 2026-11-12): "Hold minus DRV can reach
about 14.2 V if DRV hits M12_PRE before the reset completes; 2× margin." It is a project margin, not a contract
or rail number, and its 14.2 V underestimates the rail-bounded 18.34 V.

**Derived requirement.** Stated reverse-voltage rating ≥ 18.34 V × margin. The margin policy is the owner's:

| Margin | Rating needed | FJH1100 (15 V working, abs max) | ID100 / ID101 (30 V abs max) | LIS PAD / JPAD / SSTPAD / DPAD / DFN |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.0× | ≥ 18.34 V | Fails | Meets (1.64×) | No stated rating; fails |
| 1.25× | ≥ 22.9 V | Fails | Meets | Fails |
| 1.5× | ≥ 27.5 V | Fails | Meets | Fails |
| 2× (the old policy) | ≥ 36.7 V | Fails | Fails | Fails |

ID100 / ID101 caveats:
- IR max is 10 pA at 10 V, 25 °C only.
- Its Crss figure is "design reference only, not 100 % tested". That does not matter for DT2K, which has no capacitance limit.
- Pins 3 and 5 must stay unconnected.

**A general-purpose low-leakage diode could serve DT2K.** DT2K needs:
- IR ≤ 5 nA at VR ≤ 1.3 V, over temperature;
- no capacitance limit;
- VF 0.2–0.8 V at 0.5 µA.

The low-leakage switching class carries a real VRRM (typically 75–85 V) and nA-class guaranteed IR, and some
datasheets give a hot row. That could also close DT2K's temperature question, which the picoamp parts cannot. I
have retrieved no such datasheet, so no figure is claimed: a sourcing pass would need:
- VRRM or VR (a rating, not a test condition);
- IR max at a stated VR and at temperature;
- VF at µA, which is likely typical only, as with FJH1100.

## DT2C: worst-case reverse voltage is 6.6 V normal and 10.6 V with D_L open, inside FJH1100's 15 V
DT2C's cathode is t2o and its anode is t2c, which R_C (402 k) ties to TRK12N_FB. While reverse-biased, R_C carries
only leakage, so t2c ≈ fb.
- **t2o maximum.** It is VF(D_L) at the limiter current, at most about 46 µA. The only VF maximum is 1.05 V at
  50 mA. Applying it at ≤ 46 µA (VF rises with current) is a PROJECT inference. If D_L is open, t2o can reach
  the op-amp output high, which is at most P5_SUP (5.04 V).
- **fb minimum.** fb has 4.7 kΩ (RT12NC) to GND. The only low-impedance path that can pull it is CT12N 10 nF +
  RCTZ 10 kΩ from DRV, and a full rail-to-rail DRV step of 17.34 V gives at most −17.34 × 4.7 / 14.7 =
  **−5.54 V**. RT12NO (10 M to M12_PRE) moves it by about 6 mV.

| Case | VR(DT2C) | Margin to 15 V working |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | ≤ 1.05 + 5.54 = **6.6 V** | 2.27× |
| D_L open (fault) | ≤ 5.04 + 5.54 = **10.6 V** | 1.42× |

**D_L itself** sees at most |M12_PRE| + 0.5 = 12.8 V (rail-bounded). In operation it is about 9.9 V when DRV is at
M12_PRE with the clamp engaged, through the R_O : R_C divider. D_L needs no low leakage, because its leakage goes
to t2o / GND and reaches fb only through the 100 k : 402 k divider while DT2C conducts. So a general-purpose
diode with a stated rating ≥ 12.8 V × margin serves. FJH1100 (15 V) would be 1.17×.

## The window switch must move: pulling t2p to P5_SUP leaves the only retained VCM range
- **The problem.** (a2) as written pulls XT2B's + input toward P5_SUP through 100 k outside the reset window.
  With R_1 1 M from DRV and R_2 4.99 M from REF25, t2p sits near (4.98·10 + DRV·1 + 2.5·0.2) / 11.2 ≈ 3.4 V.
  That is above (V+) − 2 V = 2.98 V, the only VCM upper limit retained.
- **Corrected placement.** Pull the − input (t2m) toward M12_PRE through 100 k instead. t2m then sits near
  M12_PRE, inside the (V−) − 0.1 V lower limit. t2p stays where the difference stage puts it (about −9.6 V with
  DRV near −12 V), so t2p > t2m and the output still saturates high.
- **Open input.** The VS 8–36 V table's VCM row is not in the retained extract, and XT2B runs at about 17.3 V.
  The correction relies on the lower limit only, the same reliance U9B already has.

## The limiter current and VF spread: 37–46 µA, and every figure rests on typical VF
- **The current.** It is (V_OH − VF) / R_O:
  - maximum (5.0423 − 0.46) / 99 k = **46.3 µA**;
  - minimum (4.9259 − 0.125 − 1.05) / 101 k = **37.1 µA**.
- **Where the VF values come from.**
  - Typical VF at 45 µA is about 0.66 V, interpolated from the typical 605 mV at 10 µA and 685 mV at 100 µA.
  - The low value, 0.46 V, is typical − 0.2 V, a PROJECT inference of −2 mV/°C over 100 K.
  - The high value, 1.05 V, is the 50 mA maximum.
- **Proposed VF variants for the deck:** 0.46 / 0.66 / 1.05 V at 45 µA, labelled as a typical-based spread.
- **What the limiter level does.** It sets t2o's disabled level, and hence the release swing (Cd × ΔV) and the
  approach start.

the owner should accept or reject as such.

## Hot leakage (condition 1): a paper prediction of where rule 6(b) fails
- **Inputs.** Measured sensitivity I × 42.6 kΩ. Rule 6(b) gives 107.16 µV at the floor, minus 4.2 µV of loading
  and about 20 µV of predicted coupling (Cd(0) 2.0 pF).
- **Failure IR:** about **1.95 nA** of DT2C leakage, or 2.42 nA with no coupling. This is a prediction; the deck
  will measure it.
- **Against the 25 °C sourced case:** 3 pA, so the margin is about 650×.
- **Hot case.**
  - Board maximum: no ambient or board operating maximum is recorded. 125 °C is proposed, above the hottest
    steady-state desk junction on the board (120.1 °C, `current-state.md`) and below FJH1100's 175 °C TJ maximum.
  - Scaling (PROJECT inference, conservative): IR doubles every 7 °C, giving 3 pA × 2^(100 / 7) ≈ **60 nA**.
  - Prediction: the hot case fails rule 6(b) by about 30×. The 1.95 nA crossing is reached at about 90 °C with
    the 7 °C doubling, or 118 °C with the textbook 10 °C doubling.
- **Planned cases.**
  - DT2C-only IR ladder points at 1 nA and 2 nA on t2/adv, to measure the crossing.
  - Hot cases on t2/nom and t2/adv with all three diodes scaled.

## DT2K leakage: the 5 nA limit was a droop-budget choice, not a rule; the rule-failure IR needs the deck
- **Origin.** The requirements note said "at most 1 % of the 0.48 µA RT2B bleed at the idle hold. Leakage lowers
  the hold, the same direction as droop, which is the safe one." It protects hold droop, as a design-margin choice.
  No pass rule or contract number is behind it.
  - The 0.48 µA was an overestimate. From the recorded τ of 0.909 s and the criteria's 0.12 V droop over 50 ms,
    the bleed at the idle hold is about 0.24 µA, so "1 %" would be about 2.4 nA.
- **What DT2K leakage does.** It flows from the hold (cathode side, through the capture switch or its off path) to
  DRV, a low-impedance op-amp output.
  - It reaches TRK12N_FB only through the clamp threshold, never directly.
  - From the edge peak (about 72 ms, t2/adv attempt 1) to the end of the idle window (115 ms), it lowers the hold
    by about **0.43 mV per nA** (IR × 43 ms / 100 nF).
  - That lowers the floor DRV is clamped to by the same amount. The result is more windup (less clamp), the
    direction T2 exists to limit. It never turns the pair on.
- **Attempt 1 t2/adv margins this shifts.** DRV idle is 1.159 V below peak. Idle Vgs is 0.98 V, and idle channel
  current is 1.2 nA against a rule 10 limit of 0.54 µA. A lower floor lowers Vgs, which is the safe side for rules
  10 and 13. Rule 1 (slope 1.064 against 1.47 V/ms) is the one exposed to extra windup.
- **No recorded sensitivity converts windup into slope.** So the rule-failure IR cannot be derived on paper. I
  propose two DT2K-only ladder cases on t2/adv to measure it:
  - **80 nA**: the guaranteed BAS116L 150 °C row. If IR is monotonic in temperature (inference), it bounds every
    temperature up to 150 °C;
  - **1 µA**: to locate the crossing.
  - Prediction: 80 nA lowers the floor by about 34 mV, which is 3 % of the 1.16 V below-peak. I expect no rule
    failure, but this is unproven.
- **BAS116LT1G at the tabulated temperatures.** IR is log-linear between the guaranteed 5 nA (25 °C) and 80 nA
  (150 °C) rows at 75 V. That is a PROJECT inference, applied at a reverse bias of ≤ 1.3 V (idle is about
  0.72 V), which is a second inference.

| Temperature | IR (interpolated) | Hold / floor shift by idle | Against the 5 nA limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 25 °C | 5.0 nA (row) | 2.2 mV | Zero margin |
| 40 °C (desk ambient) | 7.0 nA | 3.0 mV | Exceeds |
| 65.6 °C (board mean, continuous) | 12.3 nA | 5.3 mV | Exceeds |
| 82.1 °C (−12 V stage, continuous) | 17.7 nA | 7.6 mV | Exceeds |
| 90.4 °C (DT2C predicted-failure temperature) | 21.3 nA | 9.2 mV | Exceeds |
| 99.4 °C (−12 V stage, let-through held) | 26.0 nA | 11.2 mV | Exceeds |
| ≤ 150 °C (flat bound) | 80 nA (row) | 34 mV | Exceeds |

  - The chord between the two rows is not provably conservative in between: diffusion-dominated leakage is concave
    in log-IR vs T, so the chord can sit below it. The flat 80 nA bound is the guaranteed alternative.
  - Recommendation: replace the 5 nA droop budget with the rule outcome of the 80 nA case. If it passes every
    rule, DT2K is covered to 150 °C on a guaranteed row, with only the 1.3 V-vs-75 V inference left.
- **DT2K's 2 pF does not matter anywhere.**
  - It couples DRV into the hold only while capturing: 2 pF / 100 nF = 2e-5, so a 10 V DRV move gives 0.2 mV.
  - It loads U9B's output beside CT12N's 10 nF.
  - It has no path to fb.
- **DT2K VF.** BAS116L has no µA row, only maxima at 1 mA and above. The model fits IS and N to the 1 mA and 10 mA
  maxima (900 / 1000 mV), giving about 0.57 V at 0.5 µA. That is an extrapolation (PROJECT inference). The floor
  depends on it directly, about 1 : 1, so a DT2K VF spread pair (±0.15 V) is worth adding beside D_L's.

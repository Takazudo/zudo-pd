# D1 T2 rev 3 is parked as a fallback: its size is out of proportion to a 0.1–0.5 % model exceedance

Owner decision, 2026-11-12. Rev 3 has no code, pre-registration or dispatch. Its draft pre-registration and the
independent review of its window paper stay as written in the project files. No verdict changes:
electrical_acceptance stays NOT ESTABLISHED, and the recorded D1 failures stay failures.

  unbounded in the model.

## Rev 3 is out of proportion, and its window as drawn is not accepted
- **Size.** −12 V alone needs 2 OPA2187 packages, 4 diodes, 11 resistors, 5 capacitors and 2 switches not yet
  designed. That is for a fix whose value is margin, not a physical fault.
- **The window comparator as drawn is not accepted.** The independent review found that the OPA2187's back-to-back
  differential input diodes could load RAMP by about 10 µA (about 0.2 V) during tracking, from about 163 ms. Its
  datasheet does not specify comparator use.
- **Timing correction.** On the re-enable decks the window reopens 0.14 ms after disable, not 5–17 ms.
- **If rev 3 is ever resumed,** those findings and the review's ten unconfirmable items must be closed first.

## E21 as recorded: a current limit replaces the no-load slope ceilings

The text was read at `512e1ec`, in `project/osc-hole-field/electrical-decisions.json` (E21, USER-CONFIRMED,
2026-11-14) and the §5 row of `design/architecture-278/integration/power-contract/pd-interface-20v.md`.

| Rail | Region | Before (2026-11-03 slope exception) | E21 (no load only) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| −12 V | \|V\| ≤ 0.75 V | ≤ 1.47 V/ms at ≤ 265 mA | charging current ≤ **350 mA** into any bank up to 180 µF; no V/ms figure |
| +5 V | V ≤ 0.45 V | ≤ 0.95 V/ms at ≤ 114 mA | charging current ≤ **180 mA** into any bank up to 120 µF; no V/ms figure |

**E21's conditions:**
1. No load only. Loaded starts keep 1.23 / 1.22 / 0.51 V/ms.
2. +12 V is on the common ramp during the step. The start-skew (≤ 0.41 ms) and one-rail rows are not relaxed.
3. Monotonic, with no reversal. The rail may lead the ideal common tracking ramp by ≤ 0.25 V and may hold flat. It
   returns to the normal tracking difference **within 15 ms of the ramp start**. Band-entry skew ≤ 2 ms still applies.
4. Above the regions, the base slope rows and every voltage-form skew row apply unchanged.

E21 does not cover injection from patched signals while the rails are low (R-INJ, OSC #59).

**Condition 3: met if the ramp start is the rail's own start, not met if it is the reference's start.**
[`e21_conditions.py`](e21_conditions.py) read the identity waveforms of run 43484213339 (identity reproduced), with
the result in [`e21-conditions-20261114.json`](e21-conditions-20261114.json).
- Neither rail reverses, and the largest lead is 0.208 V (`adv` −12 V).
- The "normal tracking difference" is taken as the median over 15–25 ms after the rail start: about 51 mV on −12 V
  and 9 mV on +5 V.
- The return time depends on the start used. The per-rail RAMP reference creeps up from about 110 ms and crosses
  50 mV at 124.9 ms (+12 V and −12 V) and 128.3 ms (+5 V). The rails, held at their pre-start bias, cross 0.2 V at
  137.7–140.0 ms.

| Deck, rail | Largest lead | Returned at | From the rail start (0.2 V) | From the reference start (50 mV target) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `nom` +5 V | 72 mV | 144.5 ms | 4.6 ms | 16.2 ms |
| `adv` +5 V | 74 mV | 144.1 ms | 4.8 ms | 15.8 ms |
| `nom` −12 V | 133 mV | 143.6 ms | 5.3 ms | 18.7 ms |
| `adv` −12 V | 208 mV | 146.4 ms | 8.4 ms | 21.5 ms |

The PD proposal meant "within 15 ms of the catch-up step". On that reading, and on the rail-start reading, every
case returns in at most 8.4 ms. Measured from the reference's start, every case misses 15 ms. E21 merged without
defining the start. The PD side reads it as the rail's own start, which matches the proposal it adopted, and records
that reading as pending the owner's confirmation.

## What changes on the PD side now that #337 is merged

- The D1 no-load ceilings in the readiness ledger become the E21 current limits, with its four conditions.
- The retained phase33 analyzers keep their historical 0.95 / 1.47 V/ms screens, so that every earlier record still
  re-analyses byte for byte. E21 is read by `e21_conditions.py` and this record.
- D1 stays a PRE-ORDER HOLD until the owner confirms the rail-start reading of condition 3. Then it meets E21 in
  simulation. electrical_acceptance stays NOT ESTABLISHED.

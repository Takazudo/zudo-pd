## Why: the clamp never engaged in any completed case
- `clamp_engaged` is false everywhere; the clamp current stays at about −0.9 nA (leakage only). The −12 V
  slope is unchanged at 1.4745 V/ms against the recorded 1.4748 V/ms. Rules 12 and 13 fail because there is no
  engagement, and rule 1 because nothing changed.
- **Mechanism (traced in hot/adv `adv-a2.txt`, matched by hand calculation).** The window element BT2W is the
  pre-registered complement of the reset shape, `0.5*(1 − tanh((v(ennd) − 1.0)·8))`. It has a soft tail. At
  v(ennd) = 1.76 V (about 90 ms), its gate is 5e-6, so the switch is about 670 kΩ. The node it pulls, t2m, sits
  behind about 830 kΩ (R_1 ∥ R_2), so the "off" switch already drags t2m from −7.2 V to −9.8 V (measured −9.81 V).
  From about 85 ms XT2B is forced high and t2o is pinned at +0.65 V. That is before DRV falls past the floor
  (DRV −9.6 V at 80 ms to −12.1 V at 110 ms) and long before the conduction onset at 137.65 ms (v(ennd) 0.65 V).
- **Attempt 1's effective window was much longer.** Its BT2G used the same tanh but in the ON sense, in series with
  499 kΩ. It stayed effectively connected down to v(ennd) ≈ 0.3 V, as attempt 1's own criteria state. Taking the
  complement of the gate threshold did not reproduce that window in effect. **This is my design error in the
  (a2) pre-registration.** Even an ideal switch at 1.0 V would have closed at about 115 ms, before the 137 ms
  conduction event.
- **Leakage and VF made no difference.** Rule 6(b) margins are +15.1 µV in every completed case, from 25 °C rows to
  1 µA DT2K. The DT2C reverse peak was 1.02 V in hot/adv, so the 3 pA row applied and no bias above 5 V overlapped
  the rule 6(b) window. The hot FAIL is the same window failure, not leakage.

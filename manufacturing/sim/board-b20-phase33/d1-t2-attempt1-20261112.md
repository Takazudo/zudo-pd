### Rule 6 and L2: DT2C couples the capture step into the summing node
- **The capture step.** At each enable, the hold leaves its reset level and the capture diode DT2K comes into
  conduction. DRV − t2hb goes from 0 to the 0.42 V diode drop over about 41–45 ms, so the difference stage output
  t2o slews by k × 0.42 ≈ 2.1 V (2.76 to 4.85 V).
- **The coupling.** DT2C uses the decks' DBODY model (CJO 20 pF, about 10 pF at 4.8 V reverse). Its capacitance
  passes the 2.1 V step through RT2C into trk12n_fb: 5.0–5.5 nA for about 4 ms, about 20 pC.
- **The effect.** On CT12N (10 nF) that is a DRV offset of 1.9 mV on t2/adv and 1.8 mV on reR6, matching
  20 pC / 10 nF. The offset shifts the enable edge. The output difference then grows during the edge: 64–80 ms on
  t2/adv, where it settles at −135 µV, and 62–66 ms on reR6, where it peaks at +0.32 mV.
- **The same current flows in the control,** where the clamp returns to GND, so it never reaches the summing node.
- **The clamp's DC function plays no part.** "Clamp never engaged" under load is true at DC (≤ 27 nA). The coupling
  is a displacement current through a reverse-biased diode.

## Correction (2026-11-12): RT2S fails its own paper check, so the coupling still needs a fix
An earlier version of this record proposed `RT2S t2c 0 10k`. It does divert the displaced charge. But while the
clamp switch is closed, it also gives trk12n_fb a 509 kΩ DC path to GND (RT2C + RT2S) through the reverse-biased
diode's node. In rev 1 that node carried no DC current.

- **The divider changes by 0.9 %.** That path is in parallel with RT12NC (4.7 kΩ), so trk12n_fb's passive balance
  shifts by about 0.9 %.
- **Disabled, at about 0.19 V,** that is about 1.8 mV on the integrator's initial state, the same size as the
  offset RT2S was meant to remove.
- **In the enable window,** fb is about −0.5 mV. The shift is about 4.6 µV, which the trk12n divider (× 9.06)
  turns into about 41 µV at the output, beyond the 10 µV resolution of rule 6.

The replacement is in [d1-t2-rev2-paper-check-20261112.md](d1-t2-rev2-paper-check-20261112.md). It is not admitted.

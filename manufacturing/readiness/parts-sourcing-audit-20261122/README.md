evidence (`survey-MANIFEST.sha256` binds their bytes). Their free-text `role`, `reason` and detail-note fields keep the

decisions, stock, price tiers, JLC quantity fields, URLs and SHA-256 values are language-neutral.

`doc/src/content/docs/research/parts-sourcing-audit-20261122.mdx`; the policy is

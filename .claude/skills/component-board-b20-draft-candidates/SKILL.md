# Board B 20 V switcher-direct draft candidates

Read all JSON files in this owner and run the central validator. Since phase 33 these records own inventory lines bound to board `board-b20-draft` through the generated projection `scripts/schgen/board_b20_components.py` (from `scripts/schgen/board_b20_spec.py`; see manufacturing/board-b-20v-switcher-direct-draft.md). The board and its PCB remain an unvalidated draft; the bundle name keeps "candidates" for link stability. Unplaced records stay in candidates.json. TI SLVSBN0C,
SBOS807E, SBOS410O and the YAGEO product sheet were retrieved and hashed on 2026-10-31; the PDFs are
not retained. REF5025AIDR is the standard grade (0.1 %, 8 ppm/C), not the high grade. OPA2187
limits differ between its 4.5-8 V and 8-36 V tables; use the table for the actual supply.
RLS12FTCR020 C163047 is a stock alternative to the PE1206 shunt; its TA-I sheet was read only
from a distributor mirror, so its manufacturer facts are UNSOURCED and its primary coverage is OPEN.
PCSR2512BR010M6 C23067428 (RSH1, RSH3) was read from a DigiKey-hosted copy of the RESI sheet, so its
facts are UNSOURCED too.

Phase 19 drew the draft lands: `HSOP-8_TI-DDA0008B`, `VSSOP-8_TI-DGK0008A` and `SOIC-8_TI-D0008A`
from the TI example board layouts; `R1206_TA-I-RLS12` from the RLS12 pad row (a project basis for
the PE1206 part, whose YAGEO sheet has no land); and `R2512_RESI-PCSR2512_Kelvin`, a four-pad Kelvin
land from the PCSR2512 figure. None is fabrication-reviewed. Phase 32 attached maximum-envelope body models (outline-maximum boxes from `scripts/pcb/generate-package-envelopes.py`; visualization only).

Phase 32 (2026-11-02) added SHORTLISTED records for the B20 draft passives that had distributor listings only: 32 YAGEO RT/AT 0.1 % resistors (per-part YAGEO sheets, hashed on the retrieval date because YAGEO generates them on request), eight UNI-ROYAL thick-film resistors decoded through the SMD-SP-001 V.7 part-number system, Vishay TNPW060310K1BEEA (document 28758), three Murata GRM1885 C0G and two Samsung CL10C C0G capacitors from their reference sheets, and GRM2165C1H273JA01D C3882413, an unplaced 0805 27 nF C0G alternative. PDFs are not retained. The UNI-ROYAL sheet gives no pulse or single-shot energy rating, so the 1206 discharge loads keep that gap. Diodes PDS760-13 C96113 (PowerDI5, DS30477) and B560C-13-F C85100 (SMC, DS13012) are unplaced D3 alternatives; neither land fits the current B20 inverter template. The FH 0603CG271J500NT 270 pF part and the 27 nF / 47 nF 0603 C0G placements still have no record.

Phase 33 (2026-11-03) bound the placed records to the inventory. C9 now uses GRM2165C1H273JA01D C3882413 (0805 27 nF C0G), C12 uses Holy Stone C1206N473J050T C5451690 (1206 47 nF C0G; the NCC catalog mirror range table does not list NP0 1206 47 nF, so its facts are UNSOURCED), the 270 pF placements use YAGEO CC0603JRNPO9BN271 C107046 instead of the FH listing, and D3 is B560C-13-F C85100 on `SMC_Diodes_B560C_C85100` (courtyard 9.9 x 6.72 mm; the phase 32 note understated the body width). PDS760-13 C96113 stays an unplaced candidate.
